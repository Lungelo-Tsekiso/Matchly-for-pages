About Matchly

Matchly was created to make following community basketball easier. Instead of searching through WhatsApp groups, messages and spreadsheets for fixtures, scores and team updates, everything can be found in one place.

Whether you're a player, coach, supporter or just someone who loves the game, Matchly helps you stay connected with what's happening on and off the court.Whether you're a player, coach, supporter or just someone who loves the game, Matchly helps you stay connected with what's happening on and off the court.

Our Story

Matchly started as a response to a common problem in community sport: fixtures shared in group chats, results scribbled on paper scoresheets, and standings that only exist in someone's personal spreadsheet. Players, coaches and family members were often left guessing when the next match was, or who was leading the table.

Matchly brings that information together in one accessible, easy-to-navigate website, built for community leagues and tournaments across basketball, soccer, netball, volleyball, rugby and athletics.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring local sports communities closer by making competition information easier to access, follow and manage, all in one place.

Who Matchly Is For

  • Basketball players
  • Coaches and team managers
  • Tournament organisers
  • Sports supporters and spectators
  • Parents and families
  • Anyone interested in joining a local competition

What You Can Do on Matchly

  • View registered teams and their details
  • Check upcoming fixtures and completed results
  • Follow live league standings and knockout brackets
  • Watch a live-score demonstration during matches
